# Next-Air-Assistant

This is a local citizen air helper. Tell it where you are and in which country, what the room is for, how full it is, what you can actually do (open windows, run a filter, stay indoors), and who is in the space. Optional health notes belong in the Active table of `knowledge/preconditions.md`. Something you only say in chat is context for the pause; it does not rewrite that file.
